# Security

Please report security problems privately to **support@wayza.com** with "Security" in the subject, not in a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. Leave out any keys, and anyone else's data.

We'll acknowledge your report, keep you posted while we fix it, and credit you if you'd like.

Problems that reach people's data, let an AI reach someone who didn't allow it, or affect anyone under 18 are treated as the most urgent. If you find a live exposure, tell us before anything else.

## In scope

- These packages, including how they verify signed answers.
- wayza.com: the REST API, MCP endpoints, agent sign-up and claim, cards, sign-in, and the Commons.
